About Me

My background, academic journey, and interests.

My Academic Journey

I am currently studying Computer Engineering at the Georgia Institute of Technology. Before transferring to Georgia Tech, I attended Bellevue College in Washington, where I developed a strong foundation in mathematics, physics, and computer science.

During my time at Bellevue College, I also worked as a mathematics tutor. This experience strengthened my ability to communicate technical ideas clearly and approach challenging problems from different perspectives.

Why Computer Engineering?

My academic interests span both software and hardware. I enjoy understanding how computer systems work, from algorithms and software development to digital logic and electronic circuits.

At Georgia Tech, I am continuing to explore these areas through coursework in data structures, digital systems, and circuit analysis. I hope to apply this knowledge to practical engineering problems and further develop my technical skills.

Career Goals

My aspirations and the steps I plan to take toward my engineering career.

My Career Interests

My primary career goal is to become an engineer who can contribute to the development of complex computing systems. I am particularly interested in software engineering, embedded systems, and computer hardware. Rather than focusing exclusively on one area, I hope to develop expertise in both software and hardware and understand how they interact within modern computers.

Short-Term Goals

During my time at Georgia Tech, I plan to strengthen my programming, problem-solving, and hardware design skills. My coursework in data structures, digital systems, and circuits provides a foundation for more advanced topics such as computer architecture, operating systems, and embedded systems.

I also intend to gain practical experience through technical projects and internships. One of my immediate goals is to secure a summer internship where I can apply classroom knowledge to real engineering challenges, collaborate with experienced engineers, and improve my professional communication skills.

Long-Term Goals

After graduation, I hope to work in a technical engineering role involving software development, hardware systems, or their integration. I want to contribute to the design and improvement of reliable, efficient computing technologies.

To achieve these goals, I plan to continue building a strong technical foundation, pursue increasingly challenging projects, and explore career opportunities that allow me to grow as an engineer. I also hope to continue learning throughout my career as technologies and engineering practices evolve.
